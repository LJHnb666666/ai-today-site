隐私政策

最后更新：2026年6月7日

AI Today（以下简称「本应用」）重视您的隐私。本政策说明我们收集哪些数据、为何收集以及如何使用。

1. 我们收集的数据

本应用仅收集运行所必需的最少数据：

  • 意见反馈 — 当您使用应用内反馈表单时，我们会存储反馈内容、您的可选邮箱地址、应用语言设置及版本号。其中语言设置仅用于帮助我们理解问题发生的语言环境，提供邮箱完全出于自愿。
  • 推送通知令牌 — 若您授予通知权限，OneSignal 会为您的设备分配一个匿名标识符，用于推送每日 AI 资讯。该标识符不与任何个人身份信息关联。
  • 无数据分析，无行为追踪 — 本应用未集成任何数据统计 SDK、广告 SDK 或行为追踪工具。

2. 数据的使用方式

  • 通过 OneSignal 推送每日 AI 新闻通知。
  • 查阅并回复您自愿提交的反馈（仅在您留有邮箱时）。
  • 基于汇总、匿名的反馈主题改进应用体验。

我们不会出售、租用或以广告目的向第三方分享您的数据。

3. 第三方服务

本应用直接集成了以下第三方服务，各自适用其独立的隐私政策：

  • Supabase
    云数据库，用于存储和分发 AI 新闻内容、每日总结及应用内反馈。
    supabase.com/privacy ↗
  • OneSignal
    推送通知服务，用于在每日 AI 新闻更新时向您发送通知。
    onesignal.com/privacy_policy ↗

4. 数据留存

  • 反馈数据：保留至您申请删除为止。
  • 推送令牌：由 OneSignal 管理，卸载应用或撤销通知权限后自动失效。
  • 本地缓存：仅存储在设备内存和 iOS UserDefaults 中，不含任何个人身份信息。

5. 您的权利与选择

  • 通知 — 可随时前往 iOS 设置 → 通知 → AI Today 关闭。
  • 删除反馈数据 — 发送邮件至下方联系地址，我们将删除您的提交记录。
  • 语言偏好 — 存储在您设备本地，仅在您主动提交反馈时一并上传，用于帮助我们理解问题所在的语言环境。

6. 内容免责声明

AI Today 对公开 AI 新闻进行聚合与摘要。我们会尽力保证内容准确，但不保证内容的完整性、准确性或实时性。

7. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向当地法律规定年龄以下的未成年人，我们不会主动收集未成年人的个人信息。

8. 政策变更

我们可能不定期更新本政策。页面顶部的「最后更新」日期反映最近一次修订。继续使用本应用即表示您接受更新后的政策。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

AI Today ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This policy describes what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

1. Data We Collect

The App collects only the minimum data required to function:

  • Feedback submissions — When you use the in-app feedback form, we store the feedback content, your optional email address, the App language setting, and App version. The language setting is included solely to help us understand the context of your feedback. Providing an email is entirely optional.
  • Push notification token — If you grant notification permission, OneSignal assigns your device an anonymous identifier so we can deliver daily AI news alerts. This identifier is not linked to any personally identifiable information.
  • No analytics or tracking — We do not include any analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or behavioral tracking library in the App.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To send daily AI news push notifications (OneSignal).
  • To read and reply to feedback you voluntarily submit.
  • To improve the App based on aggregated, non-identifiable feedback themes.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

The App directly integrates the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase
    Cloud database used to store and serve AI news content, daily summaries, and in-app feedback submissions.
    supabase.com/privacy ↗
  • OneSignal
    Push notification delivery service. Used to send a daily notification when new AI news is available.
    onesignal.com/privacy_policy ↗

4. Data Retention

  • Feedback data is retained until you request deletion.
  • Push tokens are managed by OneSignal and removed when you uninstall the App or revoke notification permission.
  • Local cache is stored only in device memory and iOS UserDefaults; no personally identifiable data is stored locally.

5. Your Rights & Choices

  • Notifications — Disable at any time via iOS Settings → Notifications → AI Today.
  • Feedback data deletion — Email us at the address below and we will delete your submission.
  • Language preference — Stored locally on your device and only transmitted when you voluntarily submit feedback, to help us understand the language context of your report.

6. Content Disclaimer

AI Today aggregates and summarizes publicly available AI news. While we strive for accuracy, we do not guarantee the completeness, accuracy, or reliability of any content displayed in the App.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age required by local law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
