Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

AI Today ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This policy describes what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

1. Data We Collect

The App collects only the minimum data required to function:

  • Feedback submissions — When you use the in-app feedback form, we store the feedback content, your optional email address, the App language setting, and App version. Providing an email is entirely optional.
  • Push notification token — If you grant notification permission, OneSignal assigns your device an anonymous identifier so we can deliver daily AI news alerts. This identifier is not linked to any personally identifiable information.
  • No analytics or tracking — We do not include any analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or behavioral tracking library in the App.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To send daily AI news push notifications (OneSignal).
  • To read and reply to feedback you voluntarily submit.
  • To improve the App based on aggregated, non-identifiable feedback themes.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services. Each service is governed by its own privacy policy, linked below.

  • Supabase
    Cloud database used to store and serve AI news content, daily summaries, and in-app feedback submissions.
    supabase.com/privacy ↗
  • OneSignal
    Push notification delivery service. Used to send a daily notification when new AI news is available.
    onesignal.com/privacy_policy ↗
  • xAI (Grok) / X Platform
    Used server-side to search and aggregate AI-related posts from X (Twitter). No user data from the App is sent to xAI.
    x.ai/privacy ↗
  • Google Gemini
    Used server-side to search and summarize AI news via Google Search Grounding. No user data from the App is sent to Google Gemini.
    policies.google.com/privacy ↗
  • DeepSeek
    Used server-side to gather Chinese-language AI news and as an editorial AI to summarize and rank stories. No user data from the App is sent to DeepSeek.
    deepseek.com/privacy_policy ↗

4. Data Retention

  • Feedback data is retained until you request deletion.
  • Push tokens are managed by OneSignal and are removed when you uninstall the App or revoke notification permission in iOS Settings.
  • News content cached on your device is stored only in memory and in standard iOS UserDefaults. No personally identifiable data is stored locally.

5. Your Rights & Choices

  • Notifications — Disable at any time via iOS Settings → Notifications → AI Today.
  • Feedback data deletion — Email us at the address below and we will delete your submission.
  • Language preference — Stored locally in iOS UserDefaults on your device only; we never receive it.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Feedback form inside the App.
